# Model catalog refresh — 2026-10-31

Version `2026-10-31.1` contains 2,906 model IDs (including 958 aliases): 186 additions and 36 removals compared with `2026-10-16.2`. These are API identities, snapshots and serving variants, not 2,906 distinct base models. No provider configuration, enabled selection or user override is changed.

The existing importer fetched Models.dev canonical/provider catalogs, OpenRouter's all-modality catalog and LiteLLM on October 31. Source hashes are recorded in `_meta.importSnapshot` and `IMPORT-REPORT.json`. The report separately lists the IDs added to and removed from the published snapshot; `removedModels` also contains rejected import candidates that were never published.

## Removal and price boundaries

Retirement handling reuses the existing policy and today's source dates. OpenAI's October 23 removals and Google's October 22/23 preview removals were checked against their [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations) and [Google](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations) schedules. Announced future shutdowns, including GPT-4.1, remain. Source-specific Grok routes use the aggregate's dated retirement metadata. User-saved routes and overrides are not deleted by this catalog update.

Three newly imported candidates were manually excluded: an unconfirmed `gpt-6-rosalind` ID, a Together placeholder with a 1,000-token limit, and an automatic Pareto router. Their reasons are in `IMPORT-REPORT.json.manualExclusions`; recheck these if a future full import proposes them again.

Official reference pricing remains owned by `sources/official-pricing.json`; new verified quotes use that registry rather than aggregate reseller rates. The registry now covers 779 published IDs. Its date marks the latest focused revision, not a fresh audit of every historical price. Existing unrelated quotes and the fixed October 16 CNY/USD conversion are retained. Gemini launch rates expire after November 30; the existing client does not automatically switch reference prices at that date. Unknown prices stay absent, including unverified free routes. No paid inference, native desktop smoke, or new transport implementation was performed.
